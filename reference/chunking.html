Split

from langchain_text_splitters import RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter
splitter = RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter(chunk_size=1000, chunk_overlap=200)

Rules of thumb

chunk_sizeCounts characters by default. ~4 chars ≈ 1 token.
Starting point~200–500 tokens (≈800–2000 chars). Tune against questions with known answers.
OverlapHelps recall for small chunks. Costs redundancy.
Embedding limitnomic-embed-text: 2K tokens. Longer input may be cut off without warning.

Trade-off

Small chunksLarge chunks
+ precise match
− fact separated from context		+ context kept
− blended vector, more prompt tokens
